  2. 2

  3. 3

Questions

Does it work with my service?
YouTube TV, and Hulu + Live TV (newer). A Peacock show can fill the breaks. More services are next.
Does SAL see my password?
No. You log in on the service's own page. SAL never touches it.
Does it record or upload anything?
No. SAL looks at the picture on your screen to notice a break, on your computer, and discards it. Nothing leaves your machine.
What does it change?
SAL changes the channel, the way you would with a remote. It doesn't change the streams, and your subscriptions and their terms are your own.
What about the three-stream limit?
SAL uses the streams your plan allows, and you can set how many.
Can I keep my team on?
Yes. Lock it, and SAL keeps it on whenever it's playing. A favorite team gets a boost in the ranking instead.
What does it cost?
Free. SAL Plus, coming soon, adds adjustable rankings, more favorites and a show or movie during breaks.
TV?
Plug your laptop into the TV with HDMI, or cast the SAL window. That's it.
