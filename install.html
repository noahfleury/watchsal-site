SAL: install guide

SAL is a remote that jumps to the best game on. Put games on in two or three tabs of your own streaming service; SAL keeps the best one on screen, changes the channel at each break and comes back when play resumes. It runs on your computer and sends out only requests for public scores and a small notice file, with no account, cookie or identifier of yours.

An early build: expect wrong calls; telling Noah about them is the point.

What you need

  • YouTube TV, Hulu + Live TV (not plain Hulu), or NBA League Pass (the last two less tested).
  • A laptop or desktop with Chrome or Brave.

Install (two minutes)

  1. Open https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/sal/jgbafehhigkocnmfeddaegeiklplkofe and click Add to Chrome (Brave too; Edge soon).
  2. Click the puzzle-piece icon beside the address bar and pin SAL. Keep one copy: remove any older one first.

The first time: the setup (ten minutes)

Click the SAL icon and follow the setup:

  1. Your services: tick the ones you pay for and check each one's Streams at once.
  2. Favorite teams, if you have any.
  3. Log in to each service: each opens in the SAL window; log in there yourself, then Done, go on.
  4. YouTube TV or Hulu: Map channels now visits each channel in your guide once (5 to 15 minutes a service; hands off). Learn as I watch finds games slower the first evening.
  5. The results, with a fix beside each channel not learned: Map again or Go to the lobby.

A service you add later: tick it in Settings.

Every session: the lobby's three steps

  1. Put a game on in each tab: Open (Open another, up to its streams), a live game in each. Use Open: tabs in other windows are not seen.
  2. Arm each tab: Next tab brings the next one forward; click the SAL icon on it. The icon shows ON and the row says Armed.
  3. Start. The window goes full screen.

Peacock: play a show and tick Use during breaks. A new tab shows its game at once; its row says Learning mark for a minute.

Logging in, service by service

  • YouTube TV: your Google account.
  • Hulu + Live TV: answer Hulu's browser prompt, pick your profile and wait for its home page before Map channels now. Click once in each Hulu tab before Start, or it plays silent; its row asks until you do. Someone else watching Hulu: set its Streams at once to 1.
  • Streams at once goes up to 5 per service: YouTube TV 3 on the base plan, Hulu + Live TV 2 (more with 4K Plus or Unlimited Screens); above 3 the computer is the limit.
  • NBA League Pass: Streams at once is 3 on premium, 1 otherwise. Open a game, press "Watch Live" and click once in the page (its row asks until you do); after Start SAL changes games in that tab.

What you'll see

Each cut dips to black and a card names the game, never the score. When every game is in a break you get the Control room (Every game is in a break) with the scores, or your Peacock show.

Move the mouse for the bar: the games numbered 1 to 3, each with Lock, then Pause, Bad cut, Report and SAL. Keys:

  • Alt+B: bad cut, right after a change you didn't want.
  • Alt+L: lock the game on screen; SAL leaves only for its breaks. Alt+1, Alt+2, Alt+3 lock the game numbered 1 to 3 on the bar. Again to unlock. (Alt+2, Alt+3, Alt+R and Alt+Shift+L: on the picture and SAL's pages only.)
  • Alt+Shift+P: pause switching; again to resume.
  • Alt+R: report a problem.
  • Alt+Shift+L: the lobby.

The lobby

Click SAL on the bar, or Lobby in the Control room; the game keeps playing. Each tab shows its game, score and picture status; Switch to… changes its game. Back to the game or Escape returns. Stop ends the session: the game on screen keeps its sound, the others are muted; arm them again next time.

Report a problem

Press Alt+R, Report on the bar, or Report a problem in the lobby. Tick what happened (Wrong channel, Cut too early, No sound...), add a line, and press Save. A small file (sal-report-…) lands in Downloads: what SAL did in the last 15 minutes and why, no video or account details. Nothing is sent by itself; email it to hello@watchsal.com.

After the night, press Copy in the lobby's Last session box (under Details) and paste it into an email, with the file from Review sessions, Export .jsonl, and a few lines on how it felt.

What to do when

  • Wrong channel: press Alt+B. If a tab says Wrong channel, put the right one on there; SAL learns it.
  • Stream stopped or No picture: open that tab and press play once (SAL takes it back), else Reload on its row; if it stays dark, Stop and start again.
  • No player: a notice page, often a blackout; SAL leaves it for that game.
  • No sound: press Escape; a crossed-out speaker on the tab means muted; click it.
  • Page changed: the site changed under SAL; report it.
  • Re-arm: its capture ended (Stop, a browser restart, or an update); click the SAL icon on it.
  • Too many streams (on Hulu, often another screen at home): close a tab, or lower that service's Streams at once in Settings.
  • Anything else: pause, report it, then carry on or Stop.

What SAL changes

SAL changes the channel, the way you would with a remote. It doesn't change the streams, and your subscriptions and their terms are your own.

SAL is independent and not affiliated with any streaming service, league or network. Your subscriptions and their terms are your own.
